## Design specificity verdict

LLM assessment: authored, not templated. The brick idea does real work (pipeline hero, titles built by a figure of the owner, figures counted in studs), and the scrapbook layer gives the hobby pages a second, warmer register. It reverts to a generic dark-card portfolio in the lower half of Experience on desktop and at the tail of Projects.

Deterministic scan: 7 findings, all the known flat-type-hierarchy false positive. In-browser detector on the live site: one real finding, the footer paragraph runs about 128 characters a line at desktop width on every page. The rest are the site's own motifs (stud baseplate, brick card tops, the rotated sign, single-line asides).

Measured on the live site: no overflow at 320, 360, 390 or 1440; no text under 4.5:1; every tab stop has a focus ring; skip link works; 79 internal links and anchors all resolve; intro plays at 2560x1440 and can be skipped three ways; LCP 204 to 292 ms and CLS near zero on a fast connection.
