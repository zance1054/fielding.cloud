About

Measure twice, cut once.

I'm co-founder and CTO of The Lobbi Corp, a three-engineer startup. I own our CI/CD and infrastructure, and I co-led Wagon Works AI, a Microsoft 365 AI workspace desktop app, to a production beta with external testers.

Until Aug 2026 I was also Software Architect on TransUnion's CI/CD team, where I spent nearly seven years supporting 23 development teams. I led the migration of 80+ applications from Docker EE (an enterprise container platform) to Kubernetes and consolidated three Jenkins instances onto Harness (a build and deployment platform).

Away from work I'm a husband, a tech enthusiast, an amateur woodworker, and a hobbyist who can't seem to pick a lane. If it can be built, I probably want to build it.
